    How to play

    • Move: WASD or arrow keys, or the joystick in the bottom-left corner on touch screens.
    • Look around: drag the map. Press C or Center to come back to the robot.
    • Open a place: tap or click a building or file, or stand near one and press Space.
    • Details: every place shows a short overview first. Tap See details for the full story.
    • Quest: press Q for the quest log. The gold arrow guides you in order: home, then education and jobs by date, then skills, then contact. Visit all 15 places to become a Portfolio Master.
    • Zoom: + / − buttons, scroll wheel, or pinch.
