How to play

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys, or the joystick in the bottom-left corner on touch screens.
  • Look around: drag the map. Press C or Center to come back to the robot.
  • Open a place: Space / tap to open — stand near a building or file and press Space, or tap / click it.
  • Play: walk up to a swing, seesaw, slide, roundabout, or bench and press Space, or tap it, to ride or sit. Press Space again to hop off (slides finish on their own).
  • Mailbox and crates: press Space near the post office mailbox to read Sumit's letter, and walk into the work-yard crates to push them around.
  • Swim: walk into a pond to swim — you move slower in the water. Walk back out onto grass to stop swimming.
  • Details: every place shows a short overview first. Tap See details for the full story.
  • Quest: press Q for the quest log. The gold arrow guides you in order: home, then education and jobs by date, then skills, then contact. Visit all 15 places to become a Portfolio Master.
  • Treasure: after visiting all 15 places, a hidden golden chest appears somewhere in town, in a different spot each time you complete the quest. Follow the gold arrow to find it.
  • Zoom: + / − buttons, scroll wheel, or pinch.
  • Night: press N or the Night button for a dark map with lit windows, glowing name letters, and fireflies.
